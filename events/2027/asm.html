Theme

The Next Decade in Australian Astronomy

Eight years after Brisbane last hosted the ASM, a significantly expanded Queensland astronomy community welcomes the ASA back to the city.

Presented jointly through QUASAR and aligned with the 2026–2035 Astronomy Decadal Plan, the meeting will set a national agenda for the decade ahead.

Dates

Primary venue

Programme structure

Four pillars for the decade ahead

Together, these connected fields support a broad national programme aligned with The Next Decade in Australian Astronomy.

  • Cosmic evolution

    Trace how stars form and die, how galaxies assemble, and how matter cycles through the intergalactic medium across cosmic time.

  • The dark Universe

    Bring surveys, theory and precision cosmology together to investigate large-scale structure, dark matter and dark energy.

  • Extreme and dynamic skies

    Explore compact objects and extreme physics through gravitational waves, electromagnetic signals and multi-messenger discovery.

  • Worlds and life

    Connect planetary formation, exoplanet characterisation and habitability with the environments created by host stars.

Community participation

Town Halls for every ASA Chapter

Dedicated Town Halls for every ASA Chapter—including the History of Australian Astronomy Chapter—will create space for collaboration and shared priorities.

Dedicated special sessions

Advanced Scientific Computing & Data Infrastructure

Modular software toolkits, heterogeneous CPU/GPU architectures, green computing, and AI in science.

Community, Culture & Diversity

Inspiring future scientists, equity initiatives, and elevating Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander astronomy.
