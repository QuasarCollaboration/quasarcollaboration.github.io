About

Named for a founder of Australian astronomy

The Harley Wood School for Astronomy honours Harley Wood, long-serving Sydney Observatory astronomer and founding figure of the ASA. Each year it brings students together immediately before the Annual Scientific Meeting.

What to expect

Talks from research leaders

Lectures pitched for an early-career audience, covering the state of the field.

Practical sessions

Practical sessions on the computational and data-driven tools shaping today's research.

Peer networking

A close-knit few days to meet peers from across Australia before the ASM begins.

Honours and Postgraduate Students.
