About the meeting

A wide-ranging programme for Australian astronomy, hosted collaboratively across Queensland.

Programme theme

The Next Decade in Australian Astronomy

Aligned with the priorities of the 2026–2035 Astronomy Decadal Plan, spanning the breadth of research across UQ, QUT, and UniSQ.

Advanced Scientific Computing & Data Infrastructure

Modular software toolkits, the shift to heterogeneous CPU/GPU architectures, green computing, and AI in science.

Community, Culture & Diversity

Inspiring future scientists, equity initiatives, and elevating Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander astronomy.

Beyond these focus areas, the programme will cover the full breadth of Australian astronomy, from stars and galaxies to cosmology, extreme physics, and planetary systems.

Nationwide reach

A programme drawing expertise and attendance from research groups right across Australia.

Venue & hosts

The primary programme runs at UQ St Lucia, with QUT and UniSQ hosting additional events and special sessions through the week.

Getting there & staying

UQ St Lucia sits on the Brisbane River, a short trip from the CBD and South Bank.

From the airport

About 30–40 minutes by taxi or rideshare, or take the Airtrain straight through to the CBD.

Getting around

Flat 50¢ fares across the Translink network make ferry, metro, and bus trips between the venue, South Bank, and the CBD simple and affordable.

Where to stay

CBD and South Bank options such as ibis Styles Brisbane Elizabeth Street, Mantra South Bank, and Rydges South Bank.
