About the school

The Harley Wood Winter School of Astronomy honours Harley Wood, the long-serving Sydney Observatory astronomer and a founding figure of the ASA. Each year it brings together students from across the country, immediately before the Annual Scientific Meeting.

Talks from research leaders

Lectures pitched squarely at an early career audience, covering the state of the field.

Hands-on modern methods

Practical, applied sessions on the computational and data-driven tools shaping today's research.

Peer networking

A close-knit few days to meet peers from across Australia before the ASM begins.

Who it's for

Honours students Postgraduate students Early-career researchers
